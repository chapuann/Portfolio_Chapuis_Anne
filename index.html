About Me

I am a science outreach specialist with a background in glaciology and more than 20 years of experience working at the interface between research, education and the public.

I help research teams and organisations make scientific knowledge easier to understand, explore and use. I work from the scientific content itself to develop the right way of sharing it - whether through writing and editing, visual and educational resources, exhibitions, games, videos or digital formats.

My experience includes working closely with researchers, coordinating outreach activities for European research projects, developing educational resources and training, and translating scientific knowledge for policy-makers, professionals, teachers, students and the general public.

I particularly enjoy working on climate, environmental and Earth sciences, where complex questions often need new ways of being explained and explored.


I work in French and English.
